Fax Relay

What this is

Send a PDF or photo to any US or Canadian fax number from this device — free, no account. Pick the file, type the number, press Send, and the transmission tape prints each step until the carrier confirms delivery. If you're offline, the fax waits on this device and goes out by itself when you're back online.

What it is not

A fax cannot be sent from a browser alone, so your document necessarily leaves this device: it travels through the relay to the fax carrier. The relay stores nothing — it streams the document through — and the carrier deletes the document when transmission ends. Your history and settings stay in this browser only. There is no server-side history, no analytics, no account. The relay's source code is published with the app.

Limits: PDF, JPEG or PNG up to 4 MB; US and Canadian numbers only; the free carrier tier is 2 faxes a month of up to 5 pages each, and it always adds a cover page that counts as one. Receiving faxes is not possible.

Install it on your home screen

  • iPhone / iPad (Safari): Share button → Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen, or Install app.
  • Desktop (Chrome or Edge): the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

What changed

Where the fax goes

Sending is carried by FaxDrop (faxdrop.com), whose free tier this app uses and whose published policy is to delete the document when transmission ends. Sends are limited by their rates (10 a minute, 500 a day). A metered Telnyx adapter exists in the relay as a fallback.

Report a problem

Tap the version stamp at the bottom of the app — or the button below — for a diagnostic report you can copy. It says in words what is wrong, so please send the report rather than a screenshot.
